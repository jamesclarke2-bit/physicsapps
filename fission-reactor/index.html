Where does the energy go?

Why does the water heat up?

The neutrons transfer energy to particles in the water through collisions.

The water heats up because its particles gain kinetic energy.

Reactor power

Back-of-envelope: every fission releases the same energy and all of the thermal power comes from fission.

Working

    About this model. A simple, qualitative model. Each fission causes k more fissions on average, and k is set by the control rods. If k is below 1 the chain reaction dies away, if k is 1 the power is steady, and if k is above 1 the power grows. Heat made in the core is carried away by water, which boils water in a second loop to turn a turbine. Thermal efficiency is 35 %. After shutdown the core still makes some heat (decay heat), so cooling must continue. Numbers are chosen for teaching, and time is slowed down.
