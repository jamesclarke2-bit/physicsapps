Equations

    About this model. Point particle, no air resistance. Ground is y = 0 and up is positive; launch from (0, h0). x = uxt, y = h0 + uyt − ½gt2, vx = ux (constant), vy = uy − gt. Flight time is the positive root of y = 0. A negative launch height is a launch from a narrow pit (its walls are ignored). Velocity arrows are drawn head to tail (vx then vy, with v as the diagonal); the acceleration arrow is drawn to one side on its own scale. Progress and settings are saved on this device only.
