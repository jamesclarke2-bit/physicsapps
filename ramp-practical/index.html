About this model. The trolley starts from rest on a ramp and accelerates at a = g (sin θ − μ cos θ), where μ is a small friction coefficient. Every error is a fixed amount (seconds, millimetres or degrees), so longer runs and bigger angles give smaller percentage uncertainties. The uncertainty in a mean is half the range of the readings. Repeating shows the spread and exposes anomalies; it does not make a faulty method accurate. The uncertainty in a gradient is half the difference between the steepest and shallowest lines that pass through the error bars.
