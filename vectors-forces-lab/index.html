Why does that angle work?

The wind adds w to the sideways part of the ball's velocity. To cancel it, the ball needs a sideways part of −w, so u sin θ = −w, which gives sin θ = w ÷ u (aimed upwind).

About these models. Golf: a flat view from above, no air drag, and the wind simply adds a sideways velocity to the ball's velocity. The numbers are illustrative, not measurements of a real shoulder. In equilibrium the clockwise moment equals the anticlockwise moment.
